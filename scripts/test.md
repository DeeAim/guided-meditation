# Morning Grounding

Allow your eyes to gently close.

[pause 4s]

Take a slow, comfortable breath in.

[pause 3s]

And release the breath without effort.

[pause 6s]

For the next few moments, there is nowhere else you need to be.

[pause 6s]

Simply notice the feeling of your body being supported beneath you.

[pause 5s]

Feel the weight of your body settling naturally.

There is nothing you need to hold up right now.

[pause 5s]

Bring your attention gently back to your breathing.

You don't need to change its rhythm.

Just notice the breath arriving...

and the breath leaving.

[pause 6s]

As you breathe out, allow your shoulders to soften.

Let your jaw relax.

And allow your hands to rest comfortably.

[pause 6s]

Notice any sounds around you.

There is no need to focus on them.

Simply let them come and go.

[pause 5s]

And if thoughts begin to appear, that's okay.

You don't need to follow them.

Gently return your attention to the feeling of your body...

and to the quiet rhythm of your breath.

[pause 7s]

Take one more slow, comfortable breath in.

[pause 3s]

And gently breathe out.

[pause 5s]

As you begin this morning, carry this sense of steadiness with you.

You can return to your breath whenever you need a moment of calm.

[pause 6s]

And when you're ready, gently open your eyes.
